Results, and how I got there

ALDO Group · Signature engagement

Proving digital media drives in-store revenue

Most retailers can't say what their digital spend does for their stores. I designed and ran an incrementality test that measured it directly.

Challenge
Show the causal link between digital media and store revenue, beyond last-click and platform-reported results.
What I did
Designed and ran a controlled incrementality test, read across customer segments, then built it into how the team set and judged budgets.
Result
Quantified digital media's causal impact on in-store revenue by segment. Earlier, as an ALDO manager, I ran $20M in annual media across the US, UK & Canada and grew digital budgets 10×.
